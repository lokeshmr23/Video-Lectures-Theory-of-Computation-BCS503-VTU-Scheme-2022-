🎯 Course Outcomes (COs)

By the end of the course, you will be able to:

CO	Outcome	Bloom Level

CO1	Apply the fundamentals of automata theory to write DFA, NFA, Epsilon-NFA and convert between them.	L3 – Apply

CO2	Prove the properties of regular languages using regular expressions.	L3/L4 – Apply/Analyze

CO3	Design context-free grammars (CFGs) and pushdown automata (PDAs) for formal languages.	L3/L6 – Apply/Create

CO4	Design Turing machines to solve computational problems.	L3/L6 – Apply/Create

CO5	Explain the concepts of decidability and undecidability.	L2 – Understand

🗂️ Modules Overview
Module	Title	Key Topics	COs	Hours

1	Introduction to Finite Automata	DFA, NFA, text search application, Epsilon-NFA	CO1	10

2	Regular Expressions and Properties of Regular Languages	Regular expressions, pumping lemma, closure properties, DFA minimization	CO1, CO2	10

3	Context-Free Grammars and Pushdown Automata	CFGs, parse trees, ambiguity, PDAs, DPDAs	CO3	10

4	Normal Forms and Properties of Context-Free Languages	Grammar simplification, Chomsky normal form, CFL pumping lemma, closure properties	CO3	10

5	Turing Machines and Undecidability	Turing machines, programming techniques, extensions, recursive and RE languages	CO4, CO5	10
