2. Course Learning Objectives (CLOs)
CLO No.	Course Learning Objective
CLO1	Introduce core concepts in automata and theory of computation.
CLO2	Identify different formal language classes and their relationships.
CLO3	Learn concepts of grammars and recognizers for different formal languages.
CLO4	Prove or disprove theorems in automata theory using their properties.
CLO5	Determine the decidability and intractability of computational problems.
3. Course Outcomes (COs)
At the end of the course, the student will be able to:
CO No.	Course Outcome Statement
CO1	Apply the fundamentals of automata theory to write DFA, NFA, Epsilon-NFA and conversion between them.
CO2	Prove the properties of regular languages using regular expressions.
CO3	Design context-free grammars (CFGs) and pushdown automata (PDAs) for formal languages.
CO4	Design Turing machines to solve the computational problems.
CO5	Explain the concepts of decidability and undecidability.
4. CO-Bloom’s Taxonomy Mapping
CO	Outcome Statement	Bloom Level	Justification
CO1	Apply the fundamentals of automata theory to write DFA, NFA, Epsilon-NFA and conversion between them.	L3 - Apply	Students construct DFA, NFA and Epsilon-NFA for given languages and convert between them.
CO2	Prove the properties of regular languages using regular expressions.	L3/L4 - Apply/Analyze	Students write regular expressions, convert between automata and expressions, and prove closure properties and non-regularity.
CO3	Design context-free grammars (CFGs) and pushdown automata (PDAs) for formal languages.	L3/L6 - Apply/Create	Students design CFGs and PDAs, remove ambiguity, simplify grammars and apply CFL properties.
CO4	Design Turing machines to solve the computational problems.	L3/L6 - Apply/Create	Students design Turing machines, including extended models, for given problems.
CO5	Explain the concepts of decidability and undecidability.	L2 - Understand	Students explain problems computers cannot solve, recursive and recursively enumerable languages and undecidability.
5. Bloom’s Taxonomy Levels Used in the Course
Level	Bloom Category	Action Verbs	Course Application
L1	Remember	Define, list, identify	Definitions of alphabet, language, DFA, NFA, grammar, PDA and Turing machine.
L2	Understand	Explain, describe, classify	Explain language classes, ambiguity, decidability and undecidability.
L3	Apply	Solve, construct, convert	Construct automata, grammars, PDAs and Turing machines; convert between models.
L4	Analyze	Compare, prove, examine	Prove languages non-regular or non-context-free; compare models and examine closure properties.
